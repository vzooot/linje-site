Linje

Stockholm's public transport, live and in 3D. An app for iPhone.

Fly through the metro network as a 3D model, watch trains and buses move in real time, ride along with one of them past the real streets and buildings, plan a journey, and find the right exit at your station.

Departures, positions, routes and journey suggestions come from open data and are estimates. They can be late, incomplete or wrong. Check signs, announcements and SL's own channels before you travel. Linje is provided as it is, without any guarantee.
