Support

Questions, problems and ideas about Linje.

Get in touch

Write to us by opening an issue on this site's page on GitHub: github.com/vzooot/linje-site/issues. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened; a screenshot helps.

Common questions

A train or bus is not where the app shows it

Trains are placed from SL's predicted times at stations, not from tracking, so a train can be a little ahead of or behind its place on the map. Buses are placed from their reported positions, which arrive a few seconds late. Treat both as estimates.

A departure time was wrong

Times come from SL's open data and change as traffic does. Check the signs at the stop and SL's own channels before you travel.

The app asks for my location

It uses it to show the stations nearest to you and to plan a trip from where you are. You can say no and pick a station or a point on the map instead. See the privacy policy.

Can I buy tickets in the app?

No. Linje shows and plans journeys; tickets are bought through SL.
